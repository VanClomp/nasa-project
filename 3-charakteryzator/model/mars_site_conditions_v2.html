Conditions at twelve Mars landing sites through the year

Measured weather from seven landers and rovers, dust from orbit, and sunlight computed from both. Every value states where it came from, and the first table shows what is still missing.

What is measured, partial or missing

Full means every season of the Mars year is covered. Partial means some seasons, sparse sampling, or an older coarse product. Hover a cell for the reason. Select a row to see that site below.

    How far to trust it

    • Pressure estimate: Viking 1's seasonal curve moved to each site's elevation. Against the six other stations the error is 2.3% rms.
    • Orbital dust: an average around each latitude circle from MGS TES, Mars years 24 to 27. Against optical depth measured from the ground it reads 0.31 lower than Viking 1, 0.02 lower than Viking 2, 0.11 higher than Phoenix.
    • Storm year: the 2001 global storm as TES saw it. Retrievals thin out when dust is thickest, so the peak is probably understated.
    • Sampling: Curiosity is every 10th sol and Perseverance every 20th. InSight is a patchwork of event windows.

    Not in here yet

    • Air temperature at Jezero and a continuous InSight record: a download script for both is in the project folder.
    • Any station weather for Spirit, Opportunity, Zhurong, Oxia Planum and Arcadia Planitia. Their rows rest on orbital dust, the pressure estimate and a 1996 climate model.
    • Slopes, rock abundance, mineralogy and ice depth for every site.
    • Current elevation and thermal inertia maps. Elevations were entered by hand from published values; surface maps are Viking-era.
